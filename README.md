## 🎛️ Synthesis Engines

- **Classic** — Traditional waveform-based synthesis
- **FM** — Frequency Modulation for complex timbres
- **Additive** — Build sounds from harmonic partials
- **Wave** — Wavetable-style shaping
- **Noise** — Texture, percussion, ambience
- **Physical** — Physical modeling for organic sounds

---

## 🎧 Effects Chain

Stack effects in any order and dial in precise control:

`Distortion` → `Bitcrush` → `Chorus` → `Delay` → `Reverb` → `Compressor`

## ❓ FAQ

**Is it really free?**
Yes, completely free. No account needed. Runs in your browser.

**Will my sounds be saved?**
Yes — KinetiTone saves your project and full sound history locally. Nothing is lost on refresh.

**What browsers are supported?**
Chrome, Firefox, and Edge. Brave may cause performance issues.

**Is MIDI support coming?**
Yes, MIDI connectivity is in development.
